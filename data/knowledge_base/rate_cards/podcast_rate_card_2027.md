## Rules
- Minimum buy: **4 episodes per show**.
- Mid-roll ads are read by the host and are the strongest performer for direct response (promo codes, vanity URLs).
- Pre-roll ads can be produced spots supplied by the advertiser.
- Q4 premium: podcast rates are **+20% for episodes released October 1 – December 31**.
- Downloads are guaranteed. If an episode delivers under 90% of the average, the shortfall is made up with bonus episodes.
