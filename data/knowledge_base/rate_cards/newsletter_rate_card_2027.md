## Placement definitions
- **Primary:** first ad unit, above the first story. Logo, headline, up to 75 words of copy, one link. Only one primary per send.
- **Secondary:** mid-newsletter unit. Headline, up to 50 words, one link.
- **Text classified:** one-line text ad in the classifieds block, up to 25 words.
- **Dedicated send:** a full email sent only about the advertiser to the whole list. Max one per newsletter per week.
