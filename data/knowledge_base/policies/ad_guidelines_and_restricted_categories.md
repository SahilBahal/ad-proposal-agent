# Ad Guidelines & Restricted Categories

> **Synthetic data.** Northline Media and every advertiser, agency, person and number in this file are fictional, created for a portfolio demo.

## Not accepted
- Political and ballot-measure advertising (candidates, PACs, issue campaigns)
- Cannabis and CBD
- Payday lenders and debt-settlement services
- Tobacco and vaping

## Accepted with restrictions
| Category | Rule |
|---|---|
| Sportsbooks / betting | Licensed operators only. **Allowed only in Playbook Weekly, Extra Innings and Sports-section display** (display must be geo-targeted to legal states). Must include 21+ and responsible gaming language. Not allowed in any other newsletter or podcast. |
| Crypto | Registered US exchanges only. Requires **Legal review (adds 5 business days)** and a risk disclaimer. Not allowed in dedicated sends. |
| Alcohol | 21+ audiences only: Playbook Weekly, Extra Innings, Field Notes. |
| Prescription drugs / telehealth (incl. weight-loss medications) | Requires **Legal review (adds 5 business days)**. Must include safety information link. No before/after claims. |
| Financial products | APYs and rates must include "as of" date and FDIC/SIPC language where applicable. |

## General creative rules
- No misleading claims, fake urgency ("only 2 left!") or competitor attacks.
- All ads are labeled "Sponsored" or "Presented by".
