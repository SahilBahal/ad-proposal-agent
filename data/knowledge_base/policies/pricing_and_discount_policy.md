# Pricing & Discount Policy (2027)

> **Synthetic data.** Northline Media and every advertiser, agency, person and number in this file are fictional, created for a portfolio demo.

## Order of calculation
1. Start with **gross rate card prices**.
2. Apply the **Q4 premium** (+20%) to newsletter and podcast line items that run October 1 – December 31.
3. Apply the **volume discount** based on total gross spend (after Q4 premium):
   - $50,000 – $99,999: **5%**
   - $100,000 – $249,999: **10%**
   - $250,000 and up: **15%**
4. Apply the **cross-channel bundle discount** of **5%** if the buy includes 3 or more channels (channels = newsletter, podcast, display/video, branded content).
5. **Total discount is capped at 20%.** Volume and bundle discounts add together (e.g., 10% + 5% = 15% off).
6. If an agency is involved, Northline pays a **15% agency commission** on the discounted gross. Net revenue to Northline = discounted gross × 0.85.

## Minimums
- Minimum campaign: **$10,000 gross**. Briefs under this should be pointed to a self-serve text classified package or declined politely.
- Podcast minimum: 4 episodes per show. Display minimum: 500,000 impressions.

## Exceptions
- Any discount above the 20% cap, free added value over 10% of the buy, or non-standard payment terms needs **VP of Sales approval**. Sellers should not promise these in a proposal.

## Make-goods
- Newsletter: if a send's open rate is more than 15% below the published average, the advertiser gets a free secondary placement.
- Podcast: delivery under 90% of guaranteed downloads is made up with bonus episodes.

## Terms
- Payment net 30. Cancellation free up to 30 days before the first flight date; 50% charge 14–30 days out; no cancellation within 14 days.
