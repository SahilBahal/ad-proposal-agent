# Case Study: Harbor & Pine Savings

> **Synthetic data.** Northline Media and every advertiser, agency, person and number in this file are fictional, created for a portfolio demo.

**Campaign:** High-yield savings launch, Feb – Mar 2026 · Pocket Markets, Money Moves, The Daily Ledger

## Results
- **4,180 new accounts opened** in 6 weeks, tracked through unique URLs
- **Cost per account: about $23**, versus the advertiser's $40 target from paid social
- Pocket Markets primary ads averaged a **3.9% click rate** (portfolio average 3.4%)
- Money Moves drove 31% of sign-ups on 16% of spend

## What worked
Host-read podcast ads in a personal finance show outperformed everything else per dollar. Readers responded to clear numbers (APY with an as-of date) over brand messaging.

## Good fit for
Banks, fintech apps, brokerages and insurance looking for sign-ups from savers aged 25–44.
