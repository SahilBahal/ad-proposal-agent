# Case Study: Brightcan Beverages

> **Synthetic data.** Northline Media and every advertiser, agency, person and number in this file are fictional, created for a portfolio demo.

**Campaign:** Holiday season, Nov – Dec 2025 · Playbook Weekly, Extra Innings, YouTube integration

## Results
- **62,000 coupon downloads** (goal: 40,000)
- YouTube integration: **512,000 views** in 30 days (14% above the guaranteed average)
- Extra Innings promo code redeemed by **2.1%** of estimated listeners
- Brand lift survey: unaided awareness up **9 points** among sports fans

## What worked
Pairing a weekly newsletter with a twice-weekly podcast created steady frequency through the season. The hosts tied the product into game-day segments naturally.

## Good fit for
Beverage, snack, apparel and streaming brands targeting sports fans 21+.
